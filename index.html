About REMON Firstline

Since 2010, REMON Firstline has been dedicated to redefining innerwear with a perfect balance of comfort, quality, and modern style. Our factory stands as a testament to precision craftsmanship, where every garment is thoughtfully designed to feel effortless against the skin while maintaining a refined aesthetic. Specializing in premium innerwear, we focus on soft fabrics, ergonomic fits, and durable construction— ensuring each piece delivers all-day comfort without compromising on style. With years of experience and a commitment to excellence, REMON Firstline continues to shape innerwear that feels as good as it looks—subtle, essential, and crafted for everyday confidence.
